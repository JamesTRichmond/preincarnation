# Geography used, and what was not surveyed

The overworld is Addison County, Vermont, and nowhere else. Empty margin on the cartogram is lake or a county seal. Screens do not continue into Chittenden, Washington, Orange, Windsor, or Rutland counties, or into New York.

This is a compressed cartogram. Shared edges are playable borders. They are not mile-accurate. Screen coordinates are not GPS.

## Opened while authoring

- Addison County, Wikipedia, geography and communities: shire town Middlebury; city Vergennes; the town list including Ferrisburgh, Weybridge, Whiting, Hancock, Granville, Leicester, and the rest; Otter Creek running south to north as the county's primary stream; Lake Dunmore in Salisbury and Leicester, inside the county; adjacent counties Chittenden, Washington, Orange, Windsor, Rutland, and (across the lake) Washington and Essex counties in New York. Snake Mountain and Mount Abraham are named there as viewed from Vermont Route 17. No quarry industry and no route numbers were on that page.
- Pulp Mill Covered Bridge, Wikipedia: crosses Otter Creek between Middlebury and Weybridge on Seymour Street; covered Burr arch, later altered; double-barreled; construction year disputed (a sign says about 1808–1820; a later study argues about 1850); National Register 10 September 1974, reference 74000200.
- USGS Geologic Names / state unit VTOcw: Chipman Formation, Weybridge Member, light-gray to cream fine-grained calcite marble with dolomitic layers, mapped in Addison County among others. That bedrock is why the quarry district is themed on cuts and lime. It is not evidence of a particular open pit, and this pack invents none by a historical name.
- A reproduction of Samuel Swift's county account (livingplaces.com excerpt): Lemon Fair rises in Orwell and runs through Shoreham, Bridport, and Cornwall into Otter Creek in Weybridge; Otter Creek flats in Cornwall, Whiting, Middlebury, and Salisbury were described as swamp with "islands."

## Placed from published local geography, not from a plat opened here

Do not treat these as surveyed positions: Button Bay, Kingsland Bay, Basin Harbor, Little Otter Creek, Lewis Creek, Dead Creek, Chimney Point and the Crown Point bridge approach, Snake Mountain, Larrabee's Point, Bartlett Falls and the New Haven River, the roadside inscribed rock east of Bristol village, Lincoln Gap, Middlebury Gap, Bread Loaf, Texas Falls, the Granville gulf, Falls of Lana, Fern Lake, the UVM Morgan Horse Farm gate in Weybridge, Mount Independence, New Haven Junction, Belden Falls, Monkton ridge, South Starksboro, Waltham.

The north edge of the Bristol district is South Starksboro up to the Chittenden line. Hancock and the Granville gulf are the east of the Ripton district, still in Addison County, behind the windwall. Leicester is inside the Salisbury district with Lake Dunmore. Cornwall, Weybridge, and Whiting are the quarry district. Addison, Panton, and Bridport are the western lakeshore. Waltham is pinched into the Vergennes–Shoreham fence, which is the least honest border in the cartogram: those two districts should not be read as sharing a long municipal line.

## Fictional, on purpose

Glass shore, glass chapel, glass weir, glass-warp pads, void seams, the drowned bell, the Button Bay cistern, the Snake Mountain vein, the spiral behind Texas Falls, the frost cellar (not a private house), the college stacks mouth, the absence tunnels under Mount Independence, the shipyard loft, the Morgan gate tack shed, the Independence shed, and every named quarry pit. The historic ground at Mount Independence, the falls, the covered bridge, and the lake are landmarks. The supernatural rooms are not claims about those places.

County seals that must stay shut: Charlotte / Chittenden to the north, Warren and the other eastern counties at the gaps, Brandon / Benson / Sudbury to the south, and every western step onto the lake bridges or the New York shore.
