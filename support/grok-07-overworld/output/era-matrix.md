# Six-era change matrix

Years 0, 14, 28, 42, 56, 70. A change is a route or an interaction, never a recolor.

## Ferrisburgh marsh crossings

- **Year 28** (route) `FER-28-BOARDWALK`: Little Otter boardwalk between the harbor green and the gauge rots. Creek Boots cross it. The village road to the east stays a road.
- **Year 42** (route) `FER-42-CEDARS`: The cedar trail from Kingsland Cedars south to the thicket is closed. Traffic uses the marsh dike instead.
- **Year 56** (route) `FER-56-CISTERN`: Button Bay dock sinks. The cistern mouth, previously Creek Boots on the shore, now wants the Void Oar.
- **Year 70** (route) `FER-70-MONKTON`: The Monkton ridge span opens, a road from Monkton Ridge Foot to West Bristol Fields that skips New Haven.

## New Haven fields

- **Year 70** (route) `VER-70-WEIR`: A glass weir at Belden Falls opens a Creek Boots span from the New Haven rim to the Vergennes falls foot.
- **Year 28** (route) `NHV-28-MEANDER`: The Otter meander crossing into Ferrisburgh goes deep and wants the Void Oar. The crossings north and south of it stay dry.
- **Year 42** (route) `NHV-42-RAIL`: The fence at New Haven Junction comes down. The siding is a south shortcut that did not exist in earlier years.
- **Year 56** (route) `NHV-56-DEPOT`: The north field cross between the elm and Brooksville hedge closes. Go south, then east. The depot board goes blank.

## Bristol ledges

- **Year 70** (route) `FER-70-MONKTON`: The Monkton ridge span opens, a road from Monkton Ridge Foot to West Bristol Fields that skips New Haven.
- **Year 28** (route) `BRI-28-CHUTE`: Washouts make two one-way descents: Rocky Dale down to the switchback, and Bartlett Throat down to the village east. Climb back on the long roads.
- **Year 42** (route) `BRI-42-SLIDE`: A landslide closes East Woods Corner into Lincoln's river road. Notch Road West, the valley road, still reaches West Lincoln.
- **Year 56** (route) `BRI-56-LEAP`: The Deer Leap switchback east closes. A new climb north to South Starksboro wants Ledge Iron.
- **Year 70** (route) `BRI-70-SEAM`: A void seam at the New Haven River bend reaches the Lincoln gap vista. Frost Notch or the Void Oar will do. The vista still does not enter Warren.

## Lincoln sugar slopes

- **Year 70** (route) `BRI-70-SEAM`: A void seam at the New Haven River bend reaches the Lincoln gap vista. Frost Notch or the Void Oar will do. The vista still does not enter Warren.
- **Year 28** (route) `LIN-28-FLUME`: The sugar flume opens as a Sugar Tap shortcut from the village west down to the flume walk. The valley road is still open.
- **Year 42** (route) `LIN-42-WASHOUT`: The valley road and both side paths between the village row and South Lincoln wash out. Only the flume, with the Sugar Tap, reaches the south slopes.
- **Year 56** (route) `LIN-56-SEAM`: The Void Oar can reach the gap vista from the Ripton windwall. Frost Notch still parts the windwall itself. Warren stays sealed.
- **Year 70** (route) `LIN-70-SWAP`: The village roads are rebuilt, so the south slopes no longer need the Sugar Tap. The flume itself rots. The dungeon under the flume remains, reached from the restored south roads.

## Western lakeshore

- **Year 28** (route) `WLS-28-BRIDGE`: The Dead Creek bridge's north span closes. The detour is east to Addison flats, then back.
- **Year 42** (route) `WLS-42-DIKE`: The goose-flats dike opens east, a wet shortcut that wants Creek Boots. Before this year the dike is not a path.
- **Year 56** (route) `WLS-56-OAR`: With the Void Oar from Chimney Point, a water span skips the broken Dead Creek bridge.
- **Year 70** (route) `WLS-70-SHORE`: A void seam under the Crown Point approach runs north along the Vermont shore to Snake Mountain's foot. It does not cross into New York.

## Vergennes falls

- **Year 14** (route) `VER-14-RACE`: The mill-race crossing from the overlook to the race opens. It is shut in year 0.
- **Year 28** (route) `VER-28-RACE`: The race is fenced again and the underworks mouth is buried. North Main and Creek Road still cross town.
- **Year 42** (route) `VER-42-RACE`: The race fence comes down on the east bank. The underworks mouth is open again. Crossing is a road until the catwalk fails in year 56.
- **Year 56** (route) `VER-56-CATWALK`: The falls catwalk collapses. East-west across the overlook wants the Falls Line. North-south streets stay open.
- **Year 70** (route) `VER-70-WEIR`: A glass weir at Belden Falls opens a Creek Boots span from the New Haven rim to the Vergennes falls foot.

## Central quarry country

- **Year 56** (route) `MID-56-PULP`: The Pulp Mill bridge and the two ledge roads beside it close for restoration. The Cornwall north lane stays open as the detour.
- **Year 28** (route) `QRY-28-CUT`: A cut collapses open between the dark cut and Cornwall's north lane. It wants the Quarry Lamp. The lime-kiln road to the west stays in the light.
- **Year 42** (route) `QRY-42-FAIR`: The Lemon Fair floods every north-south crossing in the Cornwall-Whiting block. Without the oar, go around through Salisbury, Shoreham, or Orwell.
- **Year 70** (route) `QRY-70-PIT`: The flooded pit's rim collapses. West, east, and south joins want the Void Oar. The pit screen is oar-only. Neighbors still connect around it.

## Middlebury college terraces

- **Year 14** (route) `MID-14-HILL`: Construction closes College Hill Road's east cut. Mill Street, one screen south, is the detour.
- **Year 28** (route) `MID-28-FOOT`: The falls footbridge's north span wants the Falls Line through year 41. Mill Street goes around.
- **Year 42** (route) `MID-42-ALLEY`: The footbridge is rebuilt and no longer wants the line. Printer's Alley, west into the bridge screen, now wants the Ledger Key.
- **Year 56** (route) `MID-56-PULP`: The Pulp Mill bridge and the two ledge roads beside it close for restoration. The Cornwall north lane stays open as the detour.
- **Year 70** (route) `MID-70-BANK`: The return bank's south path becomes a void seam and wants the Void Oar. Other south roads into Salisbury stay. A new glass-warp roots on the college knoll.

## Ripton high woods

- **Year 56** (route) `LIN-56-SEAM`: The Void Oar can reach the gap vista from the Ripton windwall. Frost Notch still parts the windwall itself. Warren stays sealed.
- **Year 28** (route) `RIP-28-WAYSIDE`: The wayside's south leg grows shut. Enter from the hollow or the plains and leave the way you came.
- **Year 42** (route) `RIP-42-PASTURE`: Bread Loaf's north pasture gate locks. Reach the meadow from Route 125, not from the upper plains.
- **Year 56** (route) `RIP-56-WALL`: The windwall still wants Frost Notch, but the Void Oar now parts it too. Hancock and Texas Falls stay east of that line.
- **Year 70** (route) `RIP-70-GULF`: The Granville gulf culvert drops, cutting the road from Hancock. The trail from Texas Falls wants Creek Boots. The spiral mouth behind the falls opens and wants the Falls Line.

## Shoreham orchards

- **Year 28** (route) `SHO-28-TRELLIS`: Orchard walls become trellises. The north wall wants the Orchard Ladder. The old hedge footpath closes. Center road stays a road.
- **Year 42** (interaction) `SHO-42-LOADING`: The creamery loading talk moves from the south apron to the east door. The hub is the same building. The cooperative board replaces the open office.
- **Year 56** (route) `SHO-56-WHARF`: Larrabee's wharf stair collapses into deep water and wants the Void Oar. Cove Stones is the shore path around it. The point does not become a ferry to New York.
- **Year 70** (route) `SHO-70-ORCHARD`: The oldest orchard's east hedge opens a new road onto Mount Independence's gate screen. A glass-warp roots among the old trees.

## Salisbury lakes

- **Year 28** (route) `SAL-28-SHORE`: Dunmore's west shore floods deep. Every exit wants the Void Oar. Lake Road, the Leicester river, and the east cove go around it.
- **Year 42** (route) `SAL-42-LANA`: The Falls of Lana footbridge wants the Falls Line. Both rims remain reachable without crossing.
- **Year 56** (route) `SAL-56-RAIL`: The Leicester Junction rail bed, rubble until now, opens east as a shortcut along the lake.
- **Year 70** (route) `SAL-70-WARP`: The glass-warp leaves the shard shore and re-roots at the east cove. The old pad is dead. You do not keep the new one until you stand on it.

## Orwell southern farms

- **Year 70** (route) `SHO-70-ORCHARD`: The oldest orchard's east hedge opens a new road onto Mount Independence's gate screen. A glass-warp roots among the old trees.
- **Year 28** (route) `ORW-28-HEDGE`: A farm hedge closes the middle of Orwell's north street unless you have the Orchard Ladder. North Farm to 22A to the village goes around.
- **Year 42** (route) `ORW-42-BLUFF`: The bluff path becomes one-way down toward the south county seal. Climb back through Creek Farm and the south farms.
- **Year 56** (route) `ORW-56-BAY`: The void bay seam opens for the Void Oar, a loop that stays inside Orwell. Mount Independence's fictional tunnels open the same year.
- **Year 70** (route) `ORW-70-LEDGE`: A ledge path from the south farms into the void bay wants Ledge Iron, so the bay is no longer only an oar screen.
