# Dialogue — opening hour

Spoken lines only. Ellis does not speak. Hearing any of this is optional; the same facts sit on objects.

## ione-threat

0. **Ione Pell:** Put that away. I will not talk to a knife.

## ione-arrival

0. **Ione Pell:** You're wet from the road. If that parcel is mine, the table will take it.
1. **Ione Pell:** I already have one.
2. **Ione Pell:** It came fourteen years ago. Same hand. Same twine.
3. **Ione Pell:** The paper said a second would come in a living hand, and that I would think it was the first.
4. **Ione Pell:** I didn't open mine. You can leave yours, or keep walking. I am not a gate.

## ione-pattern

0. **Ione Pell:** You opened yours. The paper was already telling the truth about a second one.
1. **Ione Pell:** I still have not opened mine. The table will take it if you want it out of your hand.

## ione-altered-threatened

0. **Ione Pell:** The knife was yesterday, for me. The calendar says fourteen years.
1. **Ione Pell:** Don't show it to me. The table is still what it was.

## ione-altered-delivered

0. **Ione Pell:** You left the parcel. Then you left the year.
1. **Ione Pell:** That was fourteen years ago. You are wearing a coat I have only seen drawn.

## ione-altered-carrying

0. **Ione Pell:** You still have it in your hand. I kept the first.
1. **Ione Pell:** I thought the second had missed the year. It has been fourteen.

## ione-altered-stranger

0. **Ione Pell:** I don't know your face.
1. **Ione Pell:** The coat, I know. It was drawn in a parcel I never opened. Fourteen years back.

## hart-arrival

0. **Hart Soll:** Hammer's on the nail, if the vein is where you're pointed.
1. **Hart Soll:** An edge is for cord and root. Hit the thread, if you hit anything. The face remembers the wrong tool.

## hart-holding

0. **Hart Soll:** Then you already have the weight. I won't add a speech to it.

## hart-altered

0. **Hart Soll:** Fourteen years. Whatever you did to the block, it kept.

Total spoken lines: 20.
