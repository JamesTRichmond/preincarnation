# Integration

This pack is not mounted on the 320×240 walk.

The county stays one person, Canvas 2D, death as a chapter, and the Marble Vein as the only plot. Saves stay in `localStorage` under `preincarnation.save.v1`. This pack does not read or write that key, and `SAVE_VERSION` is unchanged. The desk, if it is opened from a later branch, uses `preincarnation-buried-history-v1` and still does not touch the life.

`ui/desk.tsx` is the survey instrument for these eighty finds: excavate or recover, read the line, lock it, then the one next action. Putting that desk on `/` would cover the Green. It is not imported by `src/routes` or `src/components/game-app.tsx`. The handlers, the twelve scenes, and the action records are what this branch adds.

County relics in `src/game/items.ts` are not replaced. A slash still does not open the quarry.
